## About Me

I hold a bachelor's degree in Statistics and come from a mathematics background. In Ghana, the education system and modes of instruction tend to place greater emphasis on theory, with relatively limited opportunities for practical application and project-based learning outside highly specialized courses. My education in Ghana gave me a strong academic foundation, but it also showed me how important practical application and project-based learning are. I am building this portfolio to strengthen those areas through consistent practice and complete projects.

My interests lie in data analysis, data science, data engineering, and artificial intelligence and machine learning. Why these fields? I wish I could give a very profound answer but my reason is a very basic and humble one. I like data and mathematics, not too much, but just enough to appreciate their role in solving problems and to pursue a future in these fields.

I also enjoy transforming complicated, messy datasets into clear, meaningful, and easy-to-understand insights. More than anything, however, I am drawn to the idea of building a professional life that is as efficient and stress-free as possible. I do not think this is unrelated to these fields; for me, it is one of the main reasons I am interested in them.

I enjoy building things from start to finish, and learning how different parts of a data workflow fit together. Although data and technology careers can be difficult and demanding, I believe automation can help reduce unnecessary stress and make professional life more manageable. I came to understand that achieving this goal requires the application of AI and machine learning to data analysis, data science and data engineering.

## What You Will Find Here

This portfolio will include projects, notes, and practical experiments that connect statistical reasoning with modern data tools and methods. I will use the approach most appropriate to each problem rather than trying to use every tool. My aim is to understand what a tool is useful for, apply it effectively, and keep the statistical foundation behind the analysis visible.

- SQL and MySQL for exploring, cleaning, transforming, and analyzing data
- MySQL database design and relational data modeling
- Exploratory data analysis and the communication of findings
- Data cleaning workflows for incomplete, inconsistent, and messy datasets
- Reports, dashboards, and visualizations using tools such as Power BI, Tableau, and other suitable platforms
- Data engineering exercises involving repeatable workflows and automation
- Experiments with machine learning and AI applied to practical problems


## How I Approach Projects

I try to approach each project as a complete workflow rather than as an isolated exercise. This means understanding the problem, examining the data, cleaning and preparing it, carrying out the analysis, communicating the results and documenting what I learned.

I am also interested in automation because I want to work intelligently and reduce unnecessary stress in professional work. Automation is not a way for me to avoid effort. It is a way to make useful work more consistent, efficient and easier to maintain while allowing more time for judgment, problem-solving, and improvement.


## Current Focus

My current focus is developing a stronger foundation in practical data work without losing the statistical foundation of my education. Statistics continues to shape how I think about data, uncertainty, relationships and evidence.

SQL and MySQL, together with Python, are my primary tools for building practical skills, while Power BI and Tableau support the communication and visualization of results. I also have academic exposure to R, SPSS and Stata from my undergraduate studies.

These tools represent different ways of applying statistical and analytical ideas, and I may revisit them when they are appropriate for a particular problem. I am not trying to learn or use everything at once, I am building depth gradually and choosing tools with purpose. I am using projects to improve my ability to:

- Write clear and reliable SQL
- Understand relational data and database structure
- Work with imperfect real-world datasets
- Explain technical findings in simple language
- Build projects that are organized, reproducible, and useful


## Long-Term Direction

My long-term goal is to build automated systems that combine AI, machine learning, data analysis and data engineering. I want to develop systems that can handle assigned tasks with little to no human input while remaining useful, reliable and understandable.

This is an ambitious goal, and I know it will require sustained learning, practical experience and a willingness to improve through difficult problems. This portfolio is one way I am building that experience step by step.


## Why I Am Documenting This Journey

I am documenting my progress to remind myself why I started, make my learning visible and create a record of the projects I complete. I also hope that the lessons, mistakes, and solutions documented here may be useful to others who are building practical skills after a primarily theory-oriented education.

In the distant future, I hope to contribute to improving the education system in Ghana so that students can feel more accomplished and better prepared when they complete their studies.

This future goal is not an indication that I am interested in academia. I do not envision spending my entire life researching, working in laboratories, or remaining in classrooms. Instead, I want to help provide future students with some of the support and practical opportunities I needed most during my own education, while contributing to improvements in a system I have often criticized.

For now, I am focused on learning, building and sharing the work honestly, and displaying the progress I make along the way.
